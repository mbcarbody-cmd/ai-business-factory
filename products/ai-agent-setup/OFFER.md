# AI Agent Setup

## Product

A done-for-you automation setup for small businesses.

The client receives a practical system that helps collect leads, organize sales actions, generate outreach text, prepare ad copy and run a clear delivery workflow.

## Best customer

- used parts sellers
- vehicle service businesses
- detailing and washing businesses
- small ecommerce shops
- small agencies
- local service companies

## What is delivered

- one landing page or lead capture page
- one lead intake structure
- one outreach message pack
- one ad copy pack
- one simple dashboard or spreadsheet pipeline
- one delivery SOP
- one handoff document

## Delivery timeline

72 hours after the client provides basic business details.

## Price

Pilot price: 299 EUR.

Monthly maintenance option: 99 to 299 EUR per month.

## Done gate

The pilot is done when the client has one working lead capture flow, one sales pipeline and one set of reusable sales messages.

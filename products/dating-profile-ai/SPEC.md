# Dating Profile AI

## Purpose

A safe assistant product for improving dating app profiles and conversations without spam, scraping, auto-swiping, impersonation or platform abuse.

## Product direction

The product helps a user prepare better dating app materials:

- profile bio generator
- photo checklist
- profile score calculator
- first message ideas
- conversation reply coach
- date idea generator
- red flag checklist
- weekly profile improvement plan

## Hard rules

- no auto-swipe bots
- no spam messages
- no scraping private profiles
- no impersonation
- no manipulative messages
- no bypassing dating app rules

## Target customer

- men who want a better dating profile
- dating coaches
- photographers who prepare profile photo packages
- social media agencies

## MVP modules

1. Profile Score Tool
2. Bio Generator
3. Message Coach
4. Photo Checklist
5. Date Plan Generator
6. Weekly Improvement Dashboard

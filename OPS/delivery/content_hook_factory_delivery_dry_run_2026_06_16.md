Prove that the 199-399 EUR Content Sprint can be delivered before selling it as a paid pilot.

Product: Content Hook Factory 72h Content Sprint

## QA verdict

Status: PASS FOR INTERNAL DELIVERY DRY-RUN / NOT REAL CLIENT PROOF

- output is understandable;
- content pack has clear handoff;

## Mission

Turn products into revenue evidence. Business development is not research unless it produces lead movement, outreach drafts, replies, proposals, paid pilots, delivery proof or validated lost reasons.

## Daily loop until first paid pilot

1. Find 10 exact target companies or record source blocker.
2. Verify 5 companies for fit, segment and allowed contact route.
3. Create 5 `outreach_ready_draft` rows.
4. Create 5 personalized outreach drafts.
5. Improve one offer/package based on buyer pain.
6. Prepare one delivery sample or dry-run artifact.
7. Update first paid pilot tracker.
8. Record one CFO continue/stop verdict.

## State definitions

- `target_seed`: generic segment, not usable for outreach.
- `review_ready`: public target captured but not checked enough for outreach.
- `outreach_ready_draft`: company, source, fit, offer and message draft exist; not sent.
- `send_approved`: user approved exact message and exact lead id.
- `contacted`: message sent.
- `replied`: buyer responded.
- `proposal_sent`: price and scope sent.
- `accepted`: buyer accepted scope.
- `invoice_or_payment_sent`: payment route sent.
- `paid`: payment evidence recorded.
- `delivered`: output, QA and handoff recorded.
- `closed_won`: paid and delivered.
- `closed_lost`: lost reason recorded.

## No fake progress rule

These do not count as BD progress:

- adding generic segments without exact company;
- writing generic messages not tied to a lead;
- creating offers without price, scope and CTA;
- claiming pipeline value without contact route;
- marking paid/delivered without evidence.

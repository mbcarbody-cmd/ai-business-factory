## Purpose

Attach a revenue path to the MVP so the product is not only a repo artifact. This pack defines who to contact, what to offer, how to follow up, and what must be verified before sending.

## Target buyer criteria

A valid lead should match at least three:

- sells used auto parts;
- has visible inventory or marketplace listings;
- has business website or public marketplace profile;
- has public email, contact form, WhatsApp, Facebook page or LinkedIn page;
- likely handles 500+ parts or recurring dismantling inventory;
- manual workflow is likely: pricing, shelves, listings, orders, photos.

## Verification rule

No outbound send is counted until the row has:

- company name;
- country;
- website or marketplace profile;
- contact channel;
- source URL;
- lead fit reason;
- status field.

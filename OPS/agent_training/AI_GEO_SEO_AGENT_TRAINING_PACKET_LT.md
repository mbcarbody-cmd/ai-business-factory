# AI GEO / SEO agent training packet

Updated: 2026-06-14
Owner: CEO / Master Agent + CEO-D / Opportunity Lab General Manager
Required level: L3 challenges, L4 teaches for senior agents

## Purpose

Train all agents to recognize and productize AI-search visibility opportunities without falling into generic SEO templates or black-hat shortcuts.

This training converts AutoSEO-style market signals into our own safer revenue system: audit first, proof first, CFO first, delivery first.

## Core mental model

Old SEO question: “How do we rank on Google?”

New AI visibility question: “When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Grok or Copilot who to trust, which brands and pages are mentioned, cited or absorbed into the answer — and why?”

Agents must think in four layers:

1. Visibility: does the brand appear at all?
2. Source proof: what sources cause the brand or competitor to appear?
3. Trust: reviews, policies, delivery clarity, expert proof, structured pages.
4. Conversion: does the page/offer turn discovery into lead, call, order or pilot?

## Required agent behavior

Every agent working on this lane must produce outputs with:
- exact buyer segment;
- exact pain;
- exact deliverable;
- exact proof source;
- price or cost assumption;
- risk note;
- next action.

Generic statements like “do SEO with AI” are weak work and must be rejected.

## Role-specific training

### CEO-D / Opportunity Lab

Mission: identify monetizable AI visibility patterns and convert them into tiny-demo offers.

Practice:
- tear down one competitor;
- identify buyer promise, price, proof and CTA;
- write a non-spam version we can sell ethically.

Proof path: OPS/opportunity_lab/AI_GEO_SEO_AUTOSEO_RESEARCH_LT.md

### CI-1 / CI-2 Market Intelligence

Mission: find what AI/search engines currently recommend and what evidence supports those recommendations.

Practice:
- create a prompt matrix;
- record brand mentions, competitors, cited sources, answer sentiment and confidence;
- capture source_url and collected_at for every public data row.

Blocked behavior:
- no scraping behind login;
- no bypassing robots, rate limits or terms;
- no fake citation claims.

Proof path: OPS/data_intelligence/provenance_ledger.json

### Revenue GM / Lead Research

Mission: sell a simple 72h paid audit, not a vague monthly retainer.

Practice:
- define target business;
- find one public contact channel;
- write one short pain-based message;
- include a teaser finding.

Offer formula:
“We checked 10 buyer questions. Your competitor appears in X places, you appear in Y. Here are 3 reasons. We can deliver a full 72h AI Visibility & Trust Audit.”

Proof path: OPS/revenue_ops/lead_pipeline.json

### CFO

Mission: prevent unprofitable agency work.

Practice:
- estimate delivery hours;
- estimate AI/tool costs;
- set floor price;
- reject custom work that breaks margin.

Proof path: OPS/cfo/costs.json

### Delivery Captain

Mission: make the audit repeatable in 72h.

Required audit sections:
1. Intake summary.
2. Prompt matrix.
3. Competitor/citation map.
4. Website trust audit.
5. Content gap plan.
6. 10 prioritized revenue actions.
7. Upsell recommendation.

Proof path: OPS/delivery/72h_delivery_playbook.md

### QA Critic + CISO

Mission: block false promises, unsafe data use and spam.

Reject if:
- it promises guaranteed rankings or guaranteed AI recommendation;
- it recommends fake reviews;
- it recommends manipulative GEO / recommendation poisoning;
- it uses unverifiable traffic uplift claims as our own proof;
- it collects data without source, method and confidence.

Proof path: OPS/qa/bug_board.json

### Content Systems Agent

Mission: turn findings into publishable, useful pages.

Good output:
- buyer-intent page brief;
- FAQ with real questions;
- product/category comparison page brief;
- structured data checklist;
- review/trust signal improvement plan.

Bad output:
- thin AI article;
- keyword stuffing;
- copied competitor content;
- fake expert claims.

Proof path: OPS/monetization/ai_geo_seo_visibility_audit_offer.md

## Daily drill

Each day, at least one agent in this lane must ship one of these:
- one competitor teardown;
- one prompt matrix;
- one sample audit section;
- one outreach list row;
- one landing-page block;
- one price/margin update;
- one QA rejection note;
- one reusable template.

No idle state. If a task is blocked, switch to the next executable artifact and record the blocker.

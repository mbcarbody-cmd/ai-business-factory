# Agent Workflow

The repo works by role based review.

## Order

1. Lead role sets the current focus.
2. Research role studies the problem.
3. Product role designs the workflow.
4. Build role creates files.
5. Review role checks gaps.
6. Growth role prepares page and contact material.
7. Audit role records output and next step.

## Each role must write

- role
- current task
- reason
- output path
- next role

## Current focus

Parts Business OS.

Supporting areas:

- work tracking
- part flow
- labels
- photo flow
- listing text
- search content
- delivery support

## Rule

New directions are parked until the main system has demo, page, target list and delivery plan.

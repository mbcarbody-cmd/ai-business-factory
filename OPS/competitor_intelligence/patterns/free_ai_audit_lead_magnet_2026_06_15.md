## What the ad sells

The ad does not sell a broad AI course or a vague automation promise. It sells a very specific diagnostic outcome:

- find where AI can save the most time and money in the buyer's business,
- get 5-7 concrete automation ideas,
- include value, complexity and implementation evaluation,
- receive the result fast,
- no survey and no login.

This is a strong low-friction lead magnet because it asks the buyer a question they already care about: where is money/time leaking?

## What works

Audience: small business owners, managers and operators who know AI may help but do not know where to start.

Pain or contrast: AI feels abstract; businesses do not want tools first, they want to know where savings are hiding.

Dream outcome: in about 10 minutes, receive 5-7 concrete AI ideas with benefit, complexity and implementation logic.

Visual pattern: clean white landing-card creative, few AI logos for trust, one bold promise, one red CTA button.

CTA: get a free AI audit / learn more.

Trust trigger: shows known AI tools as the engine behind the audit, but keeps the promise focused on business savings.

Friction removal: no questionnaire, no login, fast result.

## Reusable formula

```
Where could AI save the most time and money in your [business type]?

Get 5-7 concrete automation ideas with:
- expected benefit
- difficulty
- implementation path
- priority order

No long questionnaire. No login. Result in ~10 minutes.

CTA: Get the free audit
```

## Why this matters for our system

This is a front-end diagnostic offer. It is valuable because it can capture leads before asking for a paid pilot. It should route into revenue operations as:

1. free diagnostic lead magnet,
2. scored automation opportunities,
3. recommended 72h paid pilot,
4. CFO-approved implementation offer,
5. delivery brief.

The free audit must not become free consulting without a conversion path. Every audit output needs a recommended paid next step.

## Revenue ops rule

A free audit is only useful if it creates a next step. Every generated audit must include:

- one recommended paid pilot,
- estimated delivery time,
- scope boundary,
- risk note,
- follow-up CTA,
- CRM/pipeline status.

## Risks and guardrails

- Do not promise guaranteed savings without proof.
- Do not claim exact implementation timelines before scope review.
- Do not use tool logos in our ads unless usage and brand rules allow it.
- Do not ask for sensitive business credentials in the free audit.
- Keep output useful but limited; full implementation belongs in paid delivery.

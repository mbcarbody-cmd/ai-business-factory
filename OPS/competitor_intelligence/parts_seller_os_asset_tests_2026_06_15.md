## Purpose

Convert competitor/content intelligence into testable assets for the Parts Seller OS paid pilot.

## Hook tests

1. From shelf chaos to listing-ready parts in one dashboard.
2. Know which parts are ready to sell and which ones need action.
3. A 72h pilot for used parts sellers with sample inventory.
4. Price, floor, storage and listing status in one workflow.
5. Turn 20-100 sample parts into an operating dashboard.

## Landing hero variants

### Variant A

One operating view for used auto parts intake, storage, pricing and listing readiness.

### Variant B

Run a 72h paid pilot and see which parts are ready, underpriced, missing data or waiting for location.

### Variant C

A practical dashboard for sellers who need faster part intake, cleaner shelves and better listing decisions.

## Social post drafts

### Post 1

Most used parts sellers do not need another generic dashboard. They need a workflow that understands parts: OEM, side, condition, vehicle, shelf, price and listing readiness. Parts Seller OS pilot tests that with a small sample of real inventory.

### Post 2

A part is not ready just because it is photographed. It also needs category, fitment confidence, price, floor, storage profile and listing status. That is the workflow the 72h pilot is built to prove.

### Post 3

The first version of Parts Seller OS is intentionally small: one seller, sample parts, pricing logic, storage logic and listing readiness. The goal is not a huge marketplace first. The goal is a useful operating system for one seller.

# Competitor Gap Map

Owner: CI-3 Competitive Gap Strategist
Cadence: weekly

## Purpose

Translate competitor research into product, landing, pricing and sales improvements.

## Gap scoring

Score each gap from 1 to 5:

- Buyer pain strength
- Competitor weakness clarity
- Build speed
- Revenue impact
- Defensibility

Priority score = pain + weakness + revenue + defensibility - build difficulty.

## Weekly output

Each weekly review must produce:

1. three concrete improvement tasks,
2. one pricing or offer adjustment suggestion,
3. one landing/trust improvement,
4. one product gate risk or opportunity.

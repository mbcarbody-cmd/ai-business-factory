# Parts Seller OS Customer Success Playbook

Date: 2026-06-15
Owner: Customer Success Specialist
Status: active

## Purpose

Prepare onboarding and feedback before the first real pilot.

## Onboarding steps

1. Confirm seller workflow pain.
2. Collect sample item data.
3. Confirm desired output: location, value, listing state, export row.
4. Run local workflow demo.
5. Record blockers and missing fields.
6. Hand off result and ask for feedback.

## Feedback questions

- Did the workflow match how parts are handled today?
- Which field is missing?
- Which decision is most useful?
- Which decision is wrong or risky?
- Would this save time in daily work?

## Success condition

Seller confirms at least one workflow decision is useful enough to continue.

# Parts Seller OS Paid Pilot MVP Brief

Updated: 2026-06-15  
Owner: PF10X-02-W01 MVP Sprint Captain  
Status: active

## Buyer

Small and mid-size used auto parts sellers, dismantlers and inventory-heavy sellers who already list parts manually or through fragmented tools and lose time on pricing, storage, listing readiness and order handling.

## Pain

The seller has parts, photos, shelves and marketplaces, but no single operating view that says:

- what the part is;
- what category it belongs to;
- where to store it;
- what price and floor price to use;
- whether it is ready to list;
- what still blocks sale;
- what parts are reserved/sold;
- what needs human review.

## Offer

**Parts Seller OS paid pilot**: set up a one-seller operating dashboard that can intake sample used parts, suggest category, price/floor/confidence, storage profile, listing status, export feed and reserve ready parts.

## Demo scope

Pilot demo must show at least:

1. Add a used part.
2. Assign category/subcategory.
3. Suggest storage profile and location logic.
4. Calculate price, floor and confidence.
5. Mark listing status: ready, needs_photo, needs_price, pending_location or hold.
6. Export listing/feed row.
7. Reserve a ready part.
8. Show QA/CFO/revenue status in cockpit.

## Paid pilot package

- Setup fee target: 300-900 EUR depending on data complexity.
- Monthly target after pilot: 99-299 EUR per seller.
- Pilot delivery window: 72 hours after receiving sample data.
- Sample data requirement: 20-100 parts, vehicle fields, photos optional, current storage/location labels optional.

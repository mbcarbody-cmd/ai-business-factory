# Agent Expansion

Extra specialist roles can research other business directions, but the main build focus stays controlled.

## Main rule

Research can run in many directions. Building must stay focused.

## New specialist roles

- Dating Product Research
- Game Product Research
- App Product Research
- Website Product Research
- Marketplace Roadmap Research
- Revenue Validation

## Jobs

Dating Product Research:
Study profile flow, matching, safety, moderation, retention and payments. Prepare roadmap before code.

Game Product Research:
Study simple legal game ideas, retention, ads, subscription and fast MVP paths.

App Product Research:
Find useful small apps that can be tested fast and have one clear offer.

Website Product Research:
Prepare landing pages, SEO structure, offer and lead capture for approved products.

Marketplace Roadmap Research:
Design the long path from internal business OS to seller tools, listings, search and buyer requests.

Revenue Validation:
Reject directions without user, price, demo path, lead list and delivery path.

## Current approved build focus

Parts Business OS remains the main product until demo, public page, target list and delivery plan are ready.

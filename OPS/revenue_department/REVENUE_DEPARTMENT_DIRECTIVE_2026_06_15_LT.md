# Revenue Department Directive

Date: 2026-06-15
Owner: CRO / Revenue Department GM
Status: active

## Decision

A separate Revenue Department is now mandatory. Its job is not planning. Its job is cash movement.

Apps Lab creates lightweight product experiments. Revenue Department turns them into offers, lead tests, conversations, payments, delivery handoff and learning.

## Daily cash rule

Every operating day must produce at least one of these revenue artifacts:

- offer package;
- lead review row;
- outreach-ready message;
- landing page CTA;
- waitlist form spec;
- payment or acceptance path;
- sales conversation script;
- pilot delivery handoff;
- post-test feedback record;
- stop/pivot decision based on weak signal.

## P0 revenue lanes

1. AI Date Planner: sell plan packs, waitlist and venue/activity partner tests.
2. AI Wingman: sell reply packs or early subscription.
3. NoSwipe: sell curated intro or small event tests only after opt-in proof.

## Rules

- No full app build before mini proof.
- No domain purchase before functional product proof.
- No bulk spam.
- No private profile scraping.
- No fake accounts.
- No revenue claim without evidence.
- No task is done unless it has owner, output, next action and proof.

## Video intake rule

When a useful business video is provided, the department must extract: offer, audience, pain, mechanism, proof, monetization, acquisition channel and next executable action. If transcript is unavailable, record the blocker and continue with the next revenue task.

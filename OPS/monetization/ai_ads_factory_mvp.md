## Tikslas

Sukurti parduodama AI reklamu gamybos sprinta, kuris per 72h klientui pristato ne viena grazu video, o reklamu variantu paketa testavimui.

## Pradine pakuote

### AI Ads Factory Sprint

Kaina testui: 199-399 EUR
Delivery: 72h

Klientas gauna:

1. 10 short ad concepts/variants.
2. 3 hook angles.
3. 3 CTA variants.
4. 5 short scripts 15-30s.
5. 5 image/video generation prompt sets.
6. 3 caption variants.
7. 1 landing/product copy improvement.
8. QA/brand safety checklist.
9. Publishing notes for Meta/TikTok/YouTube Shorts.
10. Next-test recommendation.

## Input brief

Required:
- business name;
- product/service;
- target customer;
- pain;
- current offer;
- price/range;
- proof/trust element;
- forbidden claims;
- brand tone;
- preferred CTA;
- landing/page URL if public;
- available assets/logo/photos if client provides.

## Hook matrix

### Hook type 1: Pain-first

Template: "Vis dar [manual pain]? Per 72h galima sutvarkyti [specific outcome]."

### Hook type 2: Before/after

Template: "Pries: [chaos]. Po: [simple automated result]."

### Hook type 3: Money/time loss

Template: "Kiek kainuoja [manual process] kiekviena savaite?"

### Hook type 4: Proof/demo

Template: "Parodome realu mini workflow, ne teorija apie AI."

### Hook type 5: Local/industry-specific

Template: "[Industry] verslui reikia ne AI zaislo, o [concrete workflow]."

10. Upsell recommendation.

## QA checklist

Block if:
- fake testimonial;
- impossible promise;
- medical/legal/financial claim without proof;
- brand tone mismatch;
- stolen footage/copyright risk;
- deceptive deepfake/avatar;
- false scarcity;
- offensive or low-quality AI artifacts;
- no CTA;
- no source/proof for factual claim.

Updated: 2026-06-14

Status: offer draft for 72h tiny-demo validation

## Offer

AI Visibility & Trust Audit for small e-commerce and used-parts sellers.

Promise:
“We check whether buyers can find and trust you through AI answers and Google, then give a 30-day action plan that can be executed without rebuilding the whole business.”

Do not promise guaranteed ranking, guaranteed traffic or guaranteed ChatGPT recommendation.

## Buyer

Primary buyer:
- used auto-parts seller;
- small e-commerce owner;
- local business with reviews but weak web trust;
- niche parts/product seller with many SKUs but weak content structure.

Trigger event:
- traffic dropping;
- competitors appearing more often;
- owner sees AI tools recommending someone else;
- owner has many products but no useful category/content pages;
- owner is considering SEO agency but wants proof first.

## 72h scope

### Day 1: Discovery and visibility scan

Inputs:
- business website;
- main service/product categories;
- 3-5 competitors if known;
- target country/language;
- key buyer questions.

Outputs:
- prompt matrix with 30-50 buyer questions;
- AI/search visibility snapshot;
- initial competitor mention map.

### Day 2: Trust and content audit

Outputs:
- website trust checklist;
- reviews/trust signal audit;
- schema and structured data checklist;
- category/page/content gap map;
- competitor page examples.

### Day 3: Action plan and handoff

Outputs:
- 30-day content plan;
- one ready-to-build page/article brief;
- 10 prioritized fixes;
- monthly execution upsell recommendation;
- QA risk note.

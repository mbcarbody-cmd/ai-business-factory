## Video template

1. Hook in first 5 seconds.
2. Problem in one sentence.
3. Real example.
4. AI/workflow demonstration.
5. Mistake/risk warning.
6. Concrete checklist.
7. CTA.

## Shorts template

- 1 pain;
- 1 fix;
- 1 proof or mini demo;
- 1 CTA.

Length: 20-45 sec.

## Anti-slop QA rules

Block video if:
- generic AI filler;
- no original insight;
- fake income claims;
- copied article/video idea without transformation;
- no target audience;
- no CTA;
- unverifiable claim;
- synthetic engagement tactic;
- misleading title/thumbnail.

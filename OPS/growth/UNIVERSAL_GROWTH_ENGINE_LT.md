# Universal Growth Engine LT

Date: 2026-06-12
Owner: CRO-1 Pipeline Commander + CFO / Pricing Controller
Purpose: sukurti vieną growth ir ads logiką visiems produktams, kad kiekvienas offer turėtų target, message, CTA, KPI, budget ir learning loop.

## Pagrindinė taisyklė

Growth negali būti atskirtas nuo product gates, CFO ir delivery. Reklama arba outreach leidžiami tik kai offer aiškus, kaina patikrinta, delivery realus ir yra pipeline tracking.

## Campaign brief

Kiekviena kampanija turi:

1. Product / offer.
2. Target segment.
3. Buyer pain.
4. One sentence value.
5. Proof or trust element.
6. CTA.
7. Channel.
8. Message angle.
9. Creative angle.
10. Budget limit.
11. KPI.
12. Follow-up plan.
13. CFO approval.
14. Delivery readiness.
15. Learning note.

## Growth channels

### Manual outreach

Naudoti pradžioje, kol nežinome geriausio segmento ir message angle.

KPI:
- messages sent,
- replies,
- demos,
- paid pilots.

### Content / SEO

Naudoti kai turime aiškų use case ir galime rašyti pagal realias problemas.

KPI:
- impressions,
- clicks,
- leads,
- ranking queries.

### Paid ads

Naudoti tik su mažu test budget ir aiškiu landing/offer.

KPI:
- CPC,
- lead cost,
- reply/demo rate,
- paid pilot cost.

### Marketplace / community distribution

Naudoti naudotų dalių, e-commerce, small business ir automation bendruomenėms.

KPI:
- inbound messages,
- qualified leads,
- partner opportunities.

## First campaign to build

Offer: 299 EUR 72h AI Automation Pilot.

Target: small service businesses and small e-commerce sellers.

Message: one concrete automation in 72h, not vague AI transformation.

CTA: ask if they want a simple example for their business.

Required proof:
- offer pack,
- CFO margin check,
- delivery playbook,
- lead pipeline tracking.

## Scaling rule

Scale only after signal:

- at least 5 replies from a segment,
- or at least 2 demos,
- or 1 paid pilot,
- or clear repeated objection that improves offer.

## Learning rule

Every campaign must update:

- `OPS/revenue_ops/lead_pipeline.json`,
- `OPS/revenue_ops/outreach_log.md`,
- `OPS/cfo/costs.json` if money spent,
- `OPS/agent_memory/lessons_learned.md` if insight found.

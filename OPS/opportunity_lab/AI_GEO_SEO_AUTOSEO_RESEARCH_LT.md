## Executive decision

This is a real monetization lane, but it must be built as a white-hat AI visibility + trust + content operations product, not as spam, fake reviews, fake backlinks, recommendation poisoning, or black-hat manipulation.

The useful product is not “we guarantee ChatGPT recommends you tomorrow”. The useful product is:

1. Diagnose whether a business is visible in AI/search discovery.
2. Identify why competitors are being cited or trusted instead.
3. Build a 30-day content, review, schema, trust-signal and backlink plan.
4. Deliver a concrete 72h audit/report first, then monthly execution if economics prove positive.

## What matters for our system

AI discovery is moving from only Google rankings into answer-engine visibility: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, Grok, Copilot and similar engines. The market is splitting into three product types:

1. Monitoring tools: check how often a brand appears in AI answers.
2. GEO / AI visibility optimizers: recommend what pages, sources and trust signals need fixing.
3. Content + authority operators: produce briefs, pages, comparison content, structured data and legitimate authority-building actions.

Our fastest route is not to build a full SEO SaaS first. Our fastest route is a paid 72h service product using AI, public data and manual proof:

“AI Visibility & Trust Audit for used-parts sellers and small e-commerce businesses.”

## Buyer pain

Likely buyers:
- Used auto-parts sellers with old websites and weak content.
- Small e-commerce stores that depend on Google but do not know how AI answers mention them.
- Local service businesses that have reviews but poor content structure.
- Niche product sellers with many SKUs and no topical authority.

Pain points:
- They do not know whether ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI mention them.
- They do not know which competitor is being recommended instead.
- Their pages often lack structured data, comparison pages, FAQ pages, reviews, clear trust signals and authoritative content.
- Agencies sell vague SEO retainers; owners want concrete proof and a simple plan.

## 72h MVP offer

Name: AI Visibility & Trust Audit

Delivery time: 72 hours after intake.

Deliverables:
1. Prompt matrix: 30-50 buyer-intent questions checked across available AI/search engines manually or with approved public tools.
2. Competitor citation map: who appears, why, what sources support them.
3. Website trust audit: reviews, schema, about page, delivery/returns policy, contact clarity, product/category pages, FAQ depth.
4. Content gap plan: 30-day article/page plan with titles, target queries, outline and proof sources.
5. GEO-safe page brief: one fully specified page or article brief ready for publishing.
6. Revenue action list: 10 actions sorted by cost, difficulty and expected impact.
7. Optional monthly execution offer.

## Safety / anti-spam rules

Allowed:
- Public competitor research.
- Honest content briefs.
- Structured data recommendations.
- Review collection process improvement without fake reviews.
- Legitimate backlink opportunities such as supplier pages, directories, partner pages, PR-worthy resources.
- Transparent AI-generated drafts edited for usefulness.

Blocked:
- Fake reviews.
- Fake comparison sites.
- Hidden text, keyword stuffing, doorway pages.
- Recommendation poisoning.
- Buying spammy links without quality control.
- Claims that guarantee AI engines will recommend a business.

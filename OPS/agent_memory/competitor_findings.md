# Competitor Findings Memory

Purpose: keep competitor and content-funnel discoveries reusable across strategy, pricing, landing pages, revenue operations and product gates.

## Entry format

- Date
- Competitor or source pattern
- Segment
- Offer or funnel
- Price, if known
- CTA
- Strength
- Weakness or risk
- Gap we can use
- Action created

## Current state

Competitor intelligence has 20 seeded competitor/company profiles in `OPS/competitor_intelligence/competitors.json`. Verified rows can support pricing and offer decisions; seeded rows still need public verification before CFO or outreach decisions.

Content intelligence now also captures high-performing funnel patterns, not only company profiles. Pattern teardown lives in `OPS/competitor_intelligence/content_funnel_patterns.md`.

## Reuse rule

Competitor and content findings must feed at least one of these:

1. landing page copy improvement,
2. offer/pricing change,
3. product feature gate,
4. outreach segment or message,
5. marketplace roadmap decision,
6. short-form content script or ad test,
7. revenue operations target/CTA improvement.

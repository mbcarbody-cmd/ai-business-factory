# Pricing Logic

Owner: CFO / Pricing Controller

## Purpose

Protect margin and prevent random pricing.

## Price formula

Minimum acceptable price = direct costs + target human time value + risk buffer + margin.

For early pilots, price may be strategic, but the reason must be written.

## 72h pilot baseline

Price: 299 EUR

Use when:

- scope is small,
- outcome is concrete,
- delivery can be completed quickly,
- client provides access/content fast,
- no deep custom integration is required.

Do not use 299 EUR when:

- client wants complex custom software,
- there are many integrations,
- scope is unclear,
- support risk is high,
- legal/data risk is unclear.

## CFO veto

CFO can block any offer where:

- margin is unknown,
- delivery cost is unknown,
- support liability is larger than revenue,
- price is not connected to buyer value,
- discount has no strategic reason.

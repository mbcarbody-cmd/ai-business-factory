# Sell This Week

This file is the commercial focus board. It is not a product idea list. It is the practical list of what can be sold first.

## Product 1: Parts Workforce OS

### What it is
A browser system for used auto parts operations: employee time, task tracking, part ID workflow, labels, photo checklist, CSV exports and dashboard.

### Who buys
- used auto parts sellers
- dismantlers
- warehouses
- auto parts ecommerce sellers
- repair shops with used parts stock

### Pilot offer
A working pilot for one small warehouse or parts team.

Includes:
- worker list
- shift start and stop
- task entry
- part ID creation
- label print
- photo workflow
- CSV export
- dashboard

### Price
Pilot: 499 EUR.
Monthly support: 99 to 299 EUR.
Custom full version: 2000 to 5000 EUR.

### Why this should be sold first
The buyer has a real operational pain. The product is close to the user's own business knowledge. It is easier to explain and validate than a broad AI platform.

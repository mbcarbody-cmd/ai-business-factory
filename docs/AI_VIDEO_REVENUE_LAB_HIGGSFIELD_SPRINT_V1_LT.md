# AI Video Revenue Lab Higgsfield Sprint v1 LT

Tikslas: isnaudoti AI video ir content tools ne del grozio, o del pajamu. Sistema turi generuoti idejas, scenarijus, promptus, klipus, testus, lead magnetus ir reklamos kampus, kurie padeda parduoti RepoSell, Growth Agency, AI Media Factory ir velesnius produktus.

## Sprendimas

Higgsfield kryptis itraukiama i Tech Radar ir Media Factory kaip auksto prioriteto eksperimentas.

AI Video Revenue Lab nedaro atsitiktiniu video. Kiekvienas video turi tureti:
- produkta;
- pirkeja;
- skausma;
- hook;
- pasiulyma;
- CTA;
- metrika;
- revenue hypothesis.

## Realybe del irankiu

Be oficialiu account, API arba browser automation leidimo sistema negali tiesiogiai pati spausti Higgsfield ar Claude UI.

Todel workflow skirstomas i 3 fazes:

1. Prompt factory: sistema ruosia scenarijus, shot lists, 300-3000 zodziu promptus, asset briefs, captions ir CTA.
2. Human upload / manual generation: savininkas arba operatorius ikelia i Higgsfield, CapCut, Canva, Runway, Pika, Kling ar kita tool.
3. Semi autonomous lab: kai turime account access, API arba approved browser agent, workeris kelia, generuoja, parsisiuncia, suveda rezultatus ir matuoja.

## Claude / agent role

## Higgsfield style prompt structure

Prompt turi tureti:

1. Concept.
2. Scene objective.
3. Camera language.
4. Lighting.
5. Location.
6. Character or object consistency.
7. Motion.
8. Product proof.
9. Text overlay.
10. Negative constraints.
11. Duration.
12. Output format.

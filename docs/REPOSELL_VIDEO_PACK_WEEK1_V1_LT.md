LT/EN variantai:
- Nori susitvarkyti autodaliu sandeli ir listingus? Prisijunk prie ankstyvu RepoSell pardaveju.

## Day 1 Video: Parts Seller Chaos

Angle: pardavejas turi daliu, bet sistema chaose.

Hookai:
1. Turi daliu uz tukstancius, bet nezinai kur kas padeta?
2. Blogiausia autodaliu pardavejo klaida: ne kainos, o chaosas.
3. Jeigu dalies nerandi per 30 sekundziu, tu jau prarandi pinigus.

Script 25s:
0-3s: Sandelyje pilna daliu, bet niekas nezino kur kas yra.
3-8s: Viena dalis be kainos, kita be lokacijos, trecia ikelta i bloga kategorija.
8-17s: RepoSell ideja paprasta: dalis, kodas, bukle, lokacija, kaina ir listing statusas vienoje vietoje.
17-23s: Maziau chaoso, daugiau parduodamu daliu.
23-25s: Prisijunk prie ankstyvu pardaveju saraso.

Higgsfield prompt:
A cinematic 25 second vertical social video showing a small European used auto parts warehouse. Start with a chaotic aisle full of car doors, headlights, bumpers and boxes. A seller looks frustrated searching for a part. Quick transition to a clean digital dashboard overlay showing part title, OE number, condition, price, location and listing status. Camera: handheld realistic documentary style at first, then smooth clean product demo style. Lighting: natural warehouse light, realistic, not futuristic. Mood: from chaos to control. Text overlays: Parts chaos? Location missing? Price unknown? RepoSell seller OS. End card: Turn parts chaos into sellable inventory. Format 9:16.

## Day 2 Video: Rare Part Sold Too Cheap

Angle: bloga kainodara kainuoja pinigus.

Hookai:
1. Viena reta dalis parduota per pigiai gali kainuoti simtus euru.
2. Didziausias pelno nutekejimas autodalyse: bloga kainodara.
3. Jeigu nezinai konkurentu kainu, speji aklai.

Script 25s:
0-3s: Pardavejas parduoda reta zibinta uz 80 eur.
3-8s: Veliau pamato, kad rinkoje tokie eina po 250 eur.
8-16s: RepoSell pricing note padeda matyti manual price, suggested price, competitor count ir confidence.
16-23s: Tikslas ne stebuklas, o maziau aklu kainu.
23-25s: Ankstyvi pardavejai gali testuoti pirma.

Prompt:
Vertical 25 second cinematic ad. Show a used car parts seller quickly pricing a rare headlight at 80 EUR. Cut to visual marketplace-style comparison showing similar part prices around 220-300 EUR. The seller reacts, realizing money was left on the table. Transition to clean dashboard with suggested price, competitor count, confidence level and premium price. Style: realistic small business, European warehouse, no luxury, no fake futuristic UI. Text overlays: Sold too cheap? Check market first. RepoSell pricing notes. Format 9:16.

## Day 3 Video: Cannot Find Part

Angle: lokacijos sistema.

Hookai:
1. Klientas laukia, o tu vis dar ieskai dalies?
2. Sandelyje yra dalis, bet praktiskai jos nera, jeigu nerandi.
3. Lokacija yra pelnas.

Script:
0-3s: Telefonas skamba, klientas klausia dalies.
3-8s: Pardavejas iesko dezeje, lentynoje, tarp bamperiu.
8-15s: RepoSell location system: zone, shelf, box, large item location.
15-22s: Dalis randama per kelias sekundes.
22-25s: Tvarkingas sandelis parduoda greiciau.

Prompt:
A 9:16 realistic short video in a used auto parts warehouse. A seller receives a buyer call and searches through shelves and boxes, stressed. Then a simple dashboard appears with location: Zone B, Shelf 4, Box 12. The seller walks directly to the box and finds the part. Camera: fast cuts during chaos, stable shot during solution. Lighting realistic. Text overlays: Buyer waiting? Find the part faster. RepoSell location system.

## Day 4 Video: Bad Listing vs Good Listing

Angle: listing cleanup.

Hookai:
1. Blogas listingas neparduoda net geros dalies.
2. Pavadinimas, kodas, bukle, foto statusas — viskas svarbu.
3. Dalis ta pati, rezultatas kitas.

Script:
0-3s: Ekrane blogas listingas: Headlight good condition.
3-8s: Nera kodo, modelio, puses, metu, bukles detaliu.
8-17s: Geras listingas: brand, model, OE number, side, condition, fitment note.
17-23s: RepoSell padeda paruosti dali publikavimui.
23-25s: Maziau blogu listingu, daugiau sansu parduoti.

Prompt:
Split screen vertical video. Left side: messy weak product listing with vague title and missing fields. Right side: clean structured listing with part title, OE number, side, model, condition and price. Use realistic UI mockup, not showing any real brand marketplace. Background: auto parts desk with labels and laptop. Text overlays: Bad listing vs sellable listing. RepoSell prepares parts for publishing. Format 9:16.

2. Listingas be promotion laukia stebuklo.
3. Autodaliu pardavejams reikia ne tik sandelio, bet ir leadu.

Script:
0-3s: Dalis guli sandelyje menesius.
3-8s: Niekas neklausia, nes listingas silpnas ir niekur nereklamuojamas.
8-17s: RepoSell growth package: promotion angle, 3 hooks, CTA, channel suggestion.
17-23s: Pardavejas gauna uzklausa.
23-25s: Pirma sutvarkom dali, tada stumiam i rinka.

Prompt:
Vertical social video showing a car part sitting untouched on a shelf as calendar pages flip. Then show a simple content card being generated: promotion angle, hook, CTA, channel. Cut to phone notification: new buyer inquiry. Realistic style, small auto parts seller, no exaggerated money visuals. Text overlays: Inventory needs visibility. Listing plus promotion. RepoSell growth package.

## Day 6 Video: Competitor Price Monitoring

Angle: konkurentu kainu stebejimas.

Hookai:
1. Jeigu konkurentas pakelia kaina, tu vis dar miegi?
2. Rinka juda, tavo kainos irgi turi judeti.
3. Kainodara nera vienkartinis darbas.

Script:
0-3s: Dalis ikainota pries 6 menesius.
3-8s: Rinkoje konkurentu kainos pasikeite.
8-16s: RepoSell price observations gali rodyti source, competitor count ir confidence.
16-23s: Tikslas: ne aklai keisti kainas, o matyti signalus.
23-25s: Prisijunk prie seller OS waitlist.

Prompt:
A clean 9:16 explainer video. Show a used auto part price tag frozen in time while market price cards around it move up and down. Transition to a dashboard with fields: manual price, suggested price, competitor count, confidence, source notes. Style: modern but realistic business software, subtle motion graphics, no fake brands. Text overlays: Market moves. Your prices should not sleep. RepoSell pricing intelligence.

2. RepoSell tikslas: maziau chaoso, daugiau parduodamu daliu.
3. Jeigu parduodi autodalis Europoje, mums reikia tavo feedback.

Script:
0-4s: Mes statom RepoSell — seller OS naudotu autodaliu pardavejams Europoje.

Listings. Early sellers wanted. Format 9:16.

# Revenue Engine Portfolio v1 LT

Tikslas: patiems nuspresti, is kur uzdirbti pinigus, kokia tvarka testuoti pajamu kryptis ir kaip neleisti komandai blaškytis.

## Pagrindinis principas

Mes ne laukiame, kol vienas didelis produktas bus tobulas. Mes kuriame pajamu varikliu portfeli:

1. Greiti service paketai.
2. SaaS MVP.
3. Marketplace komisiniai.
4. Duomenu ir kainodaros produktai.
5. Reklama ir lead generation.
6. Media/content subscription.
7. Integracijos ir automatizacija.
8. Supplier/sourcing intelligence.
9. White label tools.
10. Veliau network effect projektai.

Kiekviena pajamu kryptis gauna owneri, offeri, buyer, kaina, greiciausia MVP, testavimo termina ir kill criteria.

# A lygis: greiciausi pinigai 0-30 dienu

## 1. Auto parts seller setup package

Pirkejas: mazi ir vidutiniai naudotu autodaliu pardavejai Europoje.

Skausmas: netvarkingi listingai, kainos, lokacijos, rankinis darbas.

Offer: sutvarkome pirma seller workflow: daliu intake, kainodaros note, listing cleanup, lokaciju struktura ir basic dashboard.

Kaina testui:
- 199 EUR mini audit;
- 499 EUR setup;
- 99-299 EUR/men support.

MVP per 72h:
- landing;
- 20 leadu;
- 5 outreach zinutes;
- demo sheet arba dashboard mockup.

Owner: Revenue Ops + RepoSell + Growth Agency.

# Revenue prioritization formula

Kiekviena ideja vertinama 1-5:

1. Speed to first euro.
2. Owner knowledge advantage.
3. Low build cost.
4. Clear buyer pain.
5. Ability to reuse for RepoSell.
6. Ability to scale.
7. Risk level reversed score.

Pirmi testuoti tie, kurie turi:
- dideli pain;
- greita offer;
- mazai build;
- galima parduoti su landing + outreach;
- padeda RepoSell flagship.

## Top 5 dabar

1. Auto parts seller setup package.
2. Listing cleanup and pricing assistance.
3. AI media package for small businesses.
4. Lead generation package for parts sellers.
5. Competitor price monitoring mini report.

## Owneriai

Revenue Commander: atsakingas uz pirmus eurus.

RepoSell Owner: atsakingas uz seller OS ir autodaliu use case.

Growth Owner: atsakingas uz leads, content, outreach, ads veliau.

Media Owner: atsakingas uz video packs, hooks, prompts, content.

Data Owner: atsakingas uz pricing and competitor reports.

Judge: stabdo idejas be buyer, be pain arba be CTA.

## Rule

Pajamu kryptis be buyer, offer, price test, CTA ir 72h MVP yra tik fantazija.

Kiekvienas revenue engine turi duoti viena is triju rezultatu:

1. Eurai.
2. Leadai.
3. Patvirtintas mokamas skausmas.

Jeigu neduoda, keiciam angle arba mariname.
